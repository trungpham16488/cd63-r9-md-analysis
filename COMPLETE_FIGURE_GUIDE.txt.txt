------------------------------------------------

   gmx trjconv -s em.tpr -f em.trr -o cd63_r9_minimized.pdb -dump 0
   gmx trjconv -s em.tpr -f em.trr -o cd9_r9_minimized.pdb -dump 0

2. Open PyMOL and run visualization script:
   pymol cd63_r9_minimized.pdb pymol_structure_visualization.py
